Garena

Garena is a digital services company that engages in gaming, eSports, and digital entertainment. It was founded in 2009 by Forrest Li and is headquartered in Singapore. Garena operates various online games and platforms, including Free Fire, one of the most popular battle royale games globally. The company also organizes eSports tournaments and provides digital content to its users.
